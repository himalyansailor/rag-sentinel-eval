# Northwind Cloud — Incident Response

## Severity levels

A SEV1 incident is a complete outage or data loss affecting multiple customers. A SEV2 incident is a major feature degraded for many customers. A SEV3 incident is a minor issue with a workaround.

## Response targets

For SEV1 incidents the on-call engineer must acknowledge the page within 5 minutes, and customers receive a status page update within 15 minutes. SEV2 incidents must be acknowledged within 15 minutes. SEV3 incidents are handled during business hours.

## Post-incident review

Every SEV1 and SEV2 incident requires a blameless post-incident review. The written review is published to affected customers within 5 business days.
