# Northwind Cloud — Refund and Cancellation Policy

## Eligibility

Customers on monthly plans can request a full refund within 14 days of their first payment. Customers on annual plans can request a prorated refund within 30 days of the renewal date. Refunds are not available for usage-based charges such as compute hours, egress bandwidth or API overage fees.

## How to request a refund

Refund requests must be submitted from the Billing page of the Northwind console by an account owner or a billing administrator. Support agents cannot issue refunds over chat. Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method within 5 to 10 business days.

## Cancellation

Subscriptions can be cancelled at any time. Cancelling stops the next renewal; the workspace stays active until the end of the current billing period. After cancellation, data is retained for 30 days and then permanently deleted.
