# Northwind Cloud — Security and Data Protection

## Encryption

All customer data is encrypted at rest with AES-256 and in transit with TLS 1.2 or higher. Enterprise customers can bring their own encryption keys (BYOK) managed in their own cloud KMS.

## Data residency

Customer data is stored in the region selected when the workspace is created. Available regions are us-east, eu-central and ap-south. Data is never replicated outside the selected region, except for encrypted backups, which stay within the same geography.

## Backups

Workspaces are backed up every 6 hours. Backups are retained for 35 days. Point-in-time restore is available on the Enterprise plan only.

## Compliance

Northwind Cloud is SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001 certified. Penetration tests are run by an independent firm twice a year.
