# Northwind Cloud — Service Level Agreement

## Availability commitment

Northwind Cloud commits to 99.95% monthly uptime for the Business plan and 99.99% monthly uptime for the Enterprise plan. The Starter plan has no uptime commitment.

## Service credits

If monthly uptime falls below the commitment, customers receive service credits. Uptime between 99.0% and the commitment earns a credit of 10% of the monthly fee. Uptime below 99.0% earns a credit of 25% of the monthly fee. Credits must be claimed within 30 days of the end of the affected month by opening a support ticket.

## Exclusions

Scheduled maintenance announced at least 72 hours in advance does not count as downtime. Outages caused by customer misconfiguration or by force majeure events are also excluded.
